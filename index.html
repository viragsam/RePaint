RePaint.

Live-edit the fonts and colors of any loaded webpage, the way you'd fiddle with values in dev tools, but through a panel instead of the DOM inspector.

Drag me to your bookmarks bar building link...
  1. Drag the button above to your bookmarks bar (or right-click it and add it as a bookmark).
  2. Go to any page, including localhost, and click the bookmark.
  3. A panel appears top-right. Pick colors and fonts, they apply live.
  4. Click Export CSS to copy what you landed on back into your project.
  5. Optional: to use the bundled web fonts, serve this repo over HTTP and see fonts/README.md.
